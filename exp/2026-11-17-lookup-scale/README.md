# Catalog scaling to 1,000 definitions and selective risk–coverage (2026-11-17)

Addresses PVLDB review item **P1-1**: scales semantic definition lookup from the 26-definition
TPC-DS retail core (`exp/2026-11-10-lookup-pilot`) to nested enterprise catalogs
$|\mathcal M|\in\{26,100,500,1{,}000\}$ across eight warehouse domains, evaluates 390 multi-style
requests (human phrasings, unseen-generator GLM rewrites, and multi-domain queries), runs TeCoD's
released 4B embedding and 4B NLI models natively in BF16 on GPU, and characterizes the selective
wrong-match risk vs. autonomous (zero-LLM) coverage trade-off (`Figure 8`, `overleaf/gen/lookup-scale.tex`).

## 1. Benchmark Construction (`build_scale_catalog.py`, `benchmark-manifest.json`)

- **Nested catalogs** (`catalog_26.json` $\subset$ `catalog_100.json` $\subset$ `catalog_500.json` $\subset$ `catalog_1000.json`,
  `catalog_1000_sha256 = 14f949018618278eb5ad694ee24537404902a715c98f6b9f5ad35c0328a6651c`):
  - `D01..D26`: the exact 26 TPC-DS retail definitions (`store_sales`, `web_sales`, `catalog_sales`,
    `store_returns`, `inventory`).
  - `S0027..S1000`: 974 additional enterprise definitions across seven non-colliding warehouse domains
    (Subscriptions & SaaS Billing, Fulfillment & Warehouse Logistics, Payments & Settlement Ledger,
    Digital Ads & Attribution, Customer Support & Ticketing, Cloud Compute & API Metering, and
    Marketplace & Seller Operations), each with structured 5-attribute projections $B(I)=(F,e,\mathrm{AGG}_e,\tau,\varphi)$,
    homonymous business pairs, multi-facet peer families, and `pool_1000.json` (6 synthetic phrasings per
    definition = 6,000 skeleton examples for TeCoD).
  - **Strict gold invariance**: verified programmatically in `build_scale_catalog.py` that none of the
    974 added definitions alters $A(\rho)$ on any of the 96 human core requests (`requests_orig_96.json`)
    or 174 audited unseen-family GLM-4.5-Air rewrites (`requests_glm_174.json`).
- **390 multi-style evaluation requests**:
  - `orig_96` (`requests_orig_96.json`): 96 human TPC-DS core requests (single-match, homonymous `Diffs`,
    attribute-underspecified, and 17 out-of-catalog absent queries).
  - `glm_174` (`requests_glm_174.json`): 174 audited unseen-generator GLM-4.5-Air colloquial rewrites
    (`exp/2026-11-11-maint-hardening`).
  - `scale_120` (`requests_scale_120.json`): 120 multi-domain requests spanning `S0027..S1000`
    (45 exact-facet single-match, 35 homonymous/underspecified multi-match `Diffs`, and 40 out-of-catalog
    domain-adjacent absent queries).
- **Offline multi-domain synthetic training** (`train_scale_extra.jsonl`, `train_scale_ce.py`):
  - 6,738 synthetic multi-domain phrasings generated by rule-labeled templates under Definition 4.1
    (verified zero exact or normalized substring overlap against all 390 test queries), combined with
    the 26-definition synthetic training set (~12.3k total training questions / ~166k candidate pairs per seed).
  - Trained three 12-layer `BAAI/bge-reranker-base` cross-encoders with peer differences (`scale-dc-s13..15`,
    `max_len=384`, BF16, 4 epochs, batch 64, lr `2e-5`; mean `43.6 min/seed` on one NVIDIA RTX 5880 Ada GPU)
    and three without peer differences (`scale-plain-s13..15`).

## 2. Methods & Execution Environment

- **GPU server** (`10.77.50.200`, dual NVIDIA RTX 5880 Ada Generation, 49 GiB VRAM each; isolated workspace
  `/home/mingtai/lookup-scale-20261117`, `run_gpu_methods.py`):
  - Stage-1 dense recall: `BAAI/bge-m3` (`K = 8`, also measured at `K = 1, 4, 8, 16, 32`).
  - `Exact name match`, `Embedding top-1` (both oracle threshold per scale and fixed 26-def threshold).
  - `TeCoD template matcher (NLI)`: released `Qwen3-Embedding-4B` + `Qwen3-Re4B-SQL-TeCoD-TMM` running
    natively in BF16 on RTX 5880 Ada over `pool_1000.json` (6 synthetic phrasings/definition).
  - `Single CE (no peer diffs)`: both frozen 26-def (`single_ce_zero`, 1 model `s13`) and multi-domain
    refreshed (`single_ce_refreshed`, both per-seed 1-model and 3-model ensemble).
  - `MAVRA (0-retrain)`: frozen 26-definition 3-judge ensemble (`ft_base/base-dc-xl-s13..15`) with
    peer differences rendered dynamically at query time from $\mathcal M$.
  - `MAVRA (refreshed)`: multi-domain refreshed 3-judge ensemble (`ft_scale/scale-dc-s13..15`) with
    `.1/.9` abstention band and cosine fallback `0.60`.
- **Online LLM confirmation & Top-8 LLM baseline** (`noctis`, `/root/lookup-scale-20261117`, `run_llm_methods.py`):
  - Calls `DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017` (`deepseek-flash`) on `Top-8 + LLM judge` (all 390 requests × 4 scales)
    and on `Refer` candidates only for `MAVRA (0-retrain)` and `MAVRA (refreshed)`.

## 3. Summary of Results (`scale-summary.json`, `risk-coverage.json`)

### 3.1 Scaling Across $|\mathcal M| \in \{26, 100, 500, 1{,}000\}$ (`all_390` set-exact accuracy)

| Method | $\|\mathcal M\|=26$ | $100$ | $500$ | $1{,}000$ | Tokens ($1\text{k}$) | Time ($1\text{k}$) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Stage-1 `bge-m3` Recall@8 (Recall@16) | 99.2% (100.0%) | 98.7% (99.2%) | 93.8% (97.2%) | 92.0% (95.1%) | 0 | 11 ms |
| Exact name match | 48.2% | 40.8% | 34.1% | 28.7% | 0 | — |
| Embedding top-1 (oracle thr.) | 56.9% | 49.5% | 40.3% | 40.8% | 0 | 11 ms |
| TeCoD NLI (BF16 GPU, 4B+4B) | 49.2% | 36.7% | 26.2% | 22.1% | 0 | 157 ms |
| Single CE, no peer diffs (refreshed 3-CE / 1-CE) | 76.9% / 70.9% | 74.6% / 69.5% | 72.3% / 69.2% | 71.5% / 68.9% | 0 | 33 / 20 ms |
| Top-8 recall + online LLM judge | 84.4% | 89.7% | 88.2% | 86.2% | 1,026 | 2.6 s |
| MAVRA (`0-retrain`, frozen 26-def judges) | 92.0% | 85.9% | 76.1% | 72.0% | 408 | 1.76 s |
| **MAVRA (`refreshed`, peer diffs + `.1/.9`)** | **91.3%** | **90.3%** | **83.6%** | **80.8%** | **181** | **0.70 s** (35 ms GPU) |

### 3.2 Breakdown by Query Style at $|\mathcal M| = 1{,}000$

- **Human core (`orig_96`)**: Stage-1 Recall@8 stays `100.0%`; `MAVRA (0-retrain)` retains `80.2%`, and
  `MAVRA (refreshed)` reaches `91.7%` (`136` tokens, `0.44 s`; `10.3%` wrong-match risk at `70.8%` direct coverage).
- **Multi-domain (`scale_120`)**: Stage-1 Recall@8 is `99.2%`; `MAVRA (0-retrain)` reaches `83.3%` zero-shot via
  dynamically rendered peer differences, and `MAVRA (refreshed)` reaches `85.8%` (`8.3%` wrong-match risk at `70.0%`
  direct coverage; `95.8%` absent rejection).
- **Unseen-generator GLM rewrites (`glm_174`)**: Stage-1 Recall@8 drops to `82.8%` (`89.7%` Recall@16) as dense
  multi-domain near-neighbors crowd top-8, capping both `MAVRA (refreshed)` (`71.3%`) and `Top-8 + LLM judge` (`71.3%`).
