\section{\bt{Discussion and Conclusion}{讨论与结论}}
\label{sec:limitations}
\bi{\system{} turns the knowledge that individual agent sessions acquire into shared, executable definitions that every agent reuses and that are rechecked as data change. Explicit conditions and dependency versions let \system{} recheck only what an update affects, share each check and repair across definitions and agents, and execute every reused metric on a snapshot where its conditions hold. Future work will extend maintained conditions to business event identity and measurement, many-to-many joins, and incremental feature maintenance.}
{\system{} 将单个智能体会话获得的知识转化为共享的可执行定义，供所有智能体复用，并在数据变化时重新检查。显式条件与依赖版本使 \system{} 只重查更新所影响的部分，在定义和智能体之间共享每一次检查与修复，并让每个被复用的指标都在其条件成立的快照上执行。后续工作将把受维护的条件扩展到业务事件标识与度量方式、多对多连接，以及特征的增量维护。}
