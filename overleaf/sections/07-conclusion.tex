\section{\bt{Discussion and Conclusion}{讨论与结论}}
\label{sec:limitations}
\bi{A metric or feature learned in one session can serve many agents. \system{} shares executable definitions along with their versioned checks, repairs, and rewrites. Each metric query runs on a snapshot where its required conditions hold. Across \PpAgents{} TPC-DS agents, sharing reduced total token use by \PpMavraTokSaveNone\% relative to no memory. In the shared-validation experiment, increasing the group from 1 to \SvAgentsMax{} agents after one load added version reads. Revalidating all 16 definitions still took \SvStagMavraChecks{} condition checks.}
{一次会话学到的指标或特征可以供多个智能体复用。\system{} 共享可执行定义，也共享带版本的检查结果、修复和改写。每条指标查询都在满足其所需条件的快照上执行。在 \PpAgents{} 个智能体的 TPC-DS 实验中，共享使总 token 用量比无记忆时减少了 \PpMavraTokSaveNone\%。在共享验证实验中，一次增量装载后，智能体从 1 个增至 \SvAgentsMax{} 个，读取版本的次数随之增加。重新验证全部 16 个定义始终只需 \SvStagMavraChecks{} 次条件检查。}

\bi{The synthetic retail and TPC-H agent study included five types of update that broke recorded grain or join conditions. On these updates, \system{} answered \CsBreakMavra\% of questions correctly and requested clarification on the rest. All clarification requests concerned a late batch with missing ship dates. Agent-memory baselines had error rates of \CsBreakBaseWrongMin--\CsBreakBaseWrongMax\% on these five changes. Future work will address changes in business event identity and measurement, many-to-many joins, and incremental feature maintenance.}
{合成零售与 TPC-H 智能体实验包含五类破坏已记录粒度或连接条件的更新。在这五类更新上，\system{} 答对了 \CsBreakMavra\% 的问题，其余均请求澄清。所有澄清请求都涉及一批缺少发货日期的补录数据。智能体记忆基线在这五类变化上的错误率为 \CsBreakBaseWrongMin--\CsBreakBaseWrongMax\%。后续将研究如何识别业务事件标识与度量方式的变化，并支持多对多连接和特征的增量维护。}
