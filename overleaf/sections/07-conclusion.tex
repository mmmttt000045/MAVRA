\section{\bt{Discussion and Conclusion}{讨论与结论}}
\label{sec:limitations}
\bi{\system{} shares executable definitions, their checks, and validated metric repairs and rewrites across agents. Reused metrics execute on the snapshot where their conditions hold. On unchanged TPC-DS data, \PpAgents{} agents used \PpMavraTokSaveNone\% fewer tokens with shared memory than without memory. After one incremental load, the same 16 definitions together required \SvStagMavraChecks{} checks for 1--\SvAgentsMax{} agents. Version reads grew with use.}
{\system{} 在智能体之间共享可执行定义、检查结果，以及通过验证的指标修复与改写。复用指标时，查询读取满足所需条件的快照。在数据未变化的 TPC-DS 阶段，\PpAgents{} 个智能体使用共享记忆比无记忆少用 \PpMavraTokSaveNone\% 的累计 token。固定定义库所依赖的表经过一次增量装载后，在 1--\SvAgentsMax{} 个智能体的各个测试规模下，全部 16 个定义合计均需 \SvStagMavraChecks{} 次检查，版本读取次数随使用次数增加。}

\bi{Five of eleven changes on synthetic retail and TPC-H broke recorded grain or join conditions. On these five, \system{} answered \CsBreakMavra\% of questions correctly and requested clarification on the remaining \CsTpchLatekeyClarify{} because a TPC-H batch lacked ship dates. The three baselines answered \CsBreakBaseWrongMin--\CsBreakBaseWrongMax\% of the same questions incorrectly. Changing units preserved every recorded condition, yet \system{} answered \CsUnitMavraC{} of \CsUnitN{} questions correctly. Future work will address changes in business event identity and measurement, many-to-many joins, and incremental feature maintenance.}
{合成零售与 TPC-H 上的十一种变化中，有五种破坏了已记录的粒度或连接条件。在这五种变化的计分题中，\system{} 答对 \CsBreakMavra\%。其余 \CsTpchLatekeyClarify{} 题因 TPC-H 的补录批次缺少发货日期而请求澄清。三种基线在同一组题上的错答率为 \CsBreakBaseWrongMin--\CsBreakBaseWrongMax\%。度量单位变化时，所有已记录条件仍然成立，但 \system{} 在 \CsUnitN{} 题中答对 \CsUnitMavraC{} 题。后续将研究业务事件标识与度量方式的变化、多对多连接，以及特征的增量维护。}
