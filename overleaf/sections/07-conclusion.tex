\section{\bt{Conclusion}{结论}}
\label{sec:limitations}
\bi{\system{} shares parameterized metric and feature definitions extracted from verified sessions, guarded by explicit data conditions. Condition- and version-indexed validation enables selective rechecking, shared checks and repairs, and metric execution on snapshots where its conditions hold. Future work will extend maintenance to source authority and unit semantics, many-to-many joins, and incremental feature repair.}
{\system{} 共享从核验会话中提取、受显式数据条件保护的参数化指标与特征定义。按条件及表版本索引验证结果，实现选择性重查、共享检查与修复，以及在条件成立的快照上执行指标。未来工作将把维护扩展至权威数据源与量纲语义、多对多连接及特征增量修复。}
